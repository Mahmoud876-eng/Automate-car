Key System Features

Our swarm-inspired safety system combines advanced technologies to create a protective network for all road users.

Real-Time Tracking

Every vehicle, pedestrian, and cyclist is precisely located in real-time using connected vehicle technology and IoT sensors.

Swarm Intelligence

Inspired by bird flocking behavior, our central AI agent coordinates all vehicles to move safely as a protective swarm.

Safety First Intervention

The system can instantly force any vehicle to brake, slow down, or steer away when it detects danger to humans.

How SafeSwarm Works

The system follows a continuous cycle of monitoring, analysis, and intervention to prevent accidents.

1

Constant Monitoring

All vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists share their real-time position and movement data.

2

Threat Detection

The central AI analyzes all data to identify potential collision risks and dangerous situations.

3

Instant Intervention

When danger is detected, the system overrides vehicle controls to brake, slow, or steer away.

4

Swarm Coordination

All vehicles in the area are coordinated to move safely as a protective swarm around vulnerable users.
